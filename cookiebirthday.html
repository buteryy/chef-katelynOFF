Birthday Cookie Art

Celebrate Every Age, Style, and Story

Birthdays are as unique as the people we celebrate, and every cookie box is custom-designed to reflect that uniqueness.

Whether it’s a milestone age, a favorite hobby, or a beloved character, our birthday cookie boxes are crafted to bring joy, surprise, and personal meaning to the celebration.

Custom Themes for Every Personality

My birthday cookie designs span a wide range of styles and inspirations:

By Age: From sweet 17 to stylish 23, each box is tailored to the vibe of the birthday person.

General Birthday Themes: Balloons, confetti, candles, birthday cake, and cheerful colors for all ages.

Movie Themes: Like Cars, with character cookies and racing details.

Hobby Themes: Skiing, baking, music, or any passion turned into edible art.

Color Themes: Soft pastels, bold neons, or elegant monochromes.

TV Series Themes: Like The Summer I Turned Pretty, with beachy vibes and romantic icons.

Character Themes: Snoopy, anime like Fruits Basket, or any favorite figure.

Food Chain Themes: Like Chipotle, featuring custom logo cookies and fun food-inspired designs.

Beach & Ocean Themes: Seashells, waves, and tropical colors for summer birthdays.

Designed to Delight

Each birthday cookie box is a surprise waiting to happen.

I love working with clients to create and match the birthday person’s personality, interests, and favorite things—making every birthday feel truly special.
