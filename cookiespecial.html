Special Occasion Cookie Art

Celebrate Every Season with Sweet Style

Life is full of moments worth celebrating—and Chef Katelyn’s Special Occasion Cookie Art brings those moments to life with custom designs that match the spirit of each holiday.

From heartfelt tributes to festive fun, each cookie box is handcrafted to reflect the theme, colors, and joy of the season.

Mother’s Day & Father’s Day

Celebrate the love and care of parents with heartfelt messages, and personalized touches that make them feel truly appreciated.

Mother’s Day: Elegant florals, soft pastels, and hearts to honor the love and care of moms.

Father’s Day: Personalized cookies featuring dad’s favorite things—like BBQ grills, beer mugs, and even custom car logo cookies based on his favorite brand.

Graduation

Mark this milestone with cookies featuring graduation caps, diplomas, school logos, and custom names or class years. Perfect for party favors or gift boxes.

July 4th

Red, white, and blue designs with stars, fireworks, and patriotic icons—ideal for summer gatherings and Independence Day celebrations.

🎄 Christmas & 🦃 Thanksgiving

From cozy gingerbread and snowflakes to autumn leaves and pumpkins, these seasonal sets bring warmth and joy to holiday tables and gift exchanges.

💘 Valentine’s Day & 🐣 Easter

Sweet hearts, pastel bunnies, and spring florals—each set is designed to spread love, joy, and a little extra sweetness.

Whether it’s a major holiday or a personal celebration, Katelyn’s cookies are crafted to make every occasion memorable.
