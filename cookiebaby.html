Baby Shower Cookie Art

Sweet Beginnings, Beautiful Memories

Welcoming a new life is one of the most joyful moments, let’s celebrate it with cookies that are as meaningful as they are beautiful.

This collection showcases custom cookie boxes designed for surprise baby showers, planned baby showers, and baby shower gifts, each one tailored to the story of the parents-to-be.

💙 School Baby Showers – Two Baby Boys

Here’s a stunning 80-piece cookie box for two beloved teachers at my school, both expecting baby boys.

The color theme was soft light blue, with elegant and playful designs to match the occasion.

Inside the large shared box were two individual cookie boxes, each personalized for the teacher:

Biology Teacher: Featuring the school’s Panther logo and DNA strand cookies—blending science and sweetness.
Baseball Coach: Including baseball-themed cookies and the Panther logo to honor his love for the sport and school spirit.

These thoughtful touches made the surprise showers unforgettable.

💗 Pink-Themed & 💙 Blue-Themed Sample Sets

A soft pink collection designed to celebrate a baby girl, featuring delicate florals, baby icons, and gentle pastel tones.
Perfect for spring showers or gender reveal parties.

💛❤️ Yellow & Red Theme

A bold and cheerful set designed to match a vibrant baby shower theme.

This collection plays with warm tones and playful shapes, ideal for families who love color and energy.

👟 Nike Baby Shower Theme

One of the most unique sets—featuring a baby Nike shoe cookie as the centerpiece, paired with two complementary styles.

This design blends modern branding with baby charm, perfect for sporty families or sneaker-loving parents.
