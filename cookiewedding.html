Wedding & Anniversary Cookie Art

Celebrate Love with Sweet Personalization

Love deserves to be celebrated in the most thoughtful way—and what better way than with custom cookies that tell your story?

Wedding & Anniversary Cookie Art is designed to reflect the couple’s unique journey. Each cookie box is handcrafted with personalized names, dates, and themed designs that match the couple’s style, interests, or even their favorite characters.

Wedding Cookie Box

This elegant set features:

Wedding dress and tuxedo cookies

Engagement rings, champagne glasses, and floral accents

Custom name and date cookies to mark the special day

Perfect for bridal showers, wedding favors, or engagement gifts, each cookie is a sweet symbol of love and celebration.

Anniversary Cookie Box

For a fun and heartfelt anniversary, a custom cookie box featuring the beloved BT21 characters Brown and Cony, styled as a couple. This is what Cecilia & Scott used in their wedding.

This set was personalized with the couple’s names and anniversary date, blending playful charm with romantic sentiment.

Whether it’s classic elegance or quirky fun, wedding & anniversary cookies are designed to make couples feel seen, celebrated, and remembered.
